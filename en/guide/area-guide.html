Pyeong to Square Meters Explained

How to convert between pyeong and square meters, and why Korean apartment listings can look bigger than they feel.

Pyeong ↔ Square Meters: The Conversion

Official real estate documents in Korea use square meters (m²), but everyday conversation and listings still widely use pyeong, a traditional Korean unit of area.

  • 1 pyeong ≈ 3.3058 m²
  • m² → pyeong: divide the square meter value by 3.3058.
  • pyeong → m²: multiply the pyeong value by 3.3058.

For example, an apartment with 84m² of exclusive area is 84 ÷ 3.3058 ≈ 25.4 pyeong. Korea's famously common "34-pyeong" apartment size converts back to roughly 34 × 3.3058 ≈ 112.4m².

"It's listed as 34 pyeong — why does it feel smaller?"

The size advertised on a Korean apartment listing and how spacious it actually feels often don't match, because apartment area can be measured on several different bases.

  • Exclusive area (전용면적): the space a unit actually has to itself — living room, bedrooms, bathroom, kitchen, entryway.
  • Supply area (공급면적): exclusive area plus a share of shared spaces used with other units, like stairwells, hallways, and elevators.
  • Contract area (계약면적): supply area plus other shared facilities, such as the underground parking garage and management office.

Apartments are usually advertised using supply area as the "pyeong size." That means a unit marketed as "34 pyeong" has meaningfully less actual living space than that number suggests — the ratio of exclusive area to supply area for apartments is typically around 70–80%. Officetels (studio-style mixed-use units), by contrast, are often marketed using contract area and typically have a much lower ratio of 30–40%, so a "30-pyeong" officetel can feel dramatically smaller to live in than a "30-pyeong" apartment.

When comparing listings, comparing the exclusive area in m² directly is a more reliable way to judge actual living space than comparing the advertised pyeong size alone.

Other Area Units Around the World

  • Square feet (ft²): widely used in the US and UK. 1m² ≈ 10.76ft².
  • Acre: used for larger land parcels. 1 acre ≈ 4,047m² ≈ 1,224 pyeong.
  • Hectare (ha): used for farmland and large tracts. 1 hectare = 10,000m² ≈ 3,025 pyeong.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Can I use "pyeong" on official documents?

Official real estate contracts and property registries use square meters (m²) as the standard. "Pyeong" is a conventional unit used in everyday speech and advertising rather than formal paperwork.

Q. Which area figure determines the sale price?

Price is often quoted "per pyeong" based on the supply area, but comparing exclusive area is a better way to judge actual value — at the same advertised pyeong size, an apartment with a higher exclusive-area ratio gives you more usable space.

Q. Does exclusive area include the balcony?

Balconies are technically classified as "service area" and excluded from exclusive area, though many units enclose and convert balcony space into part of the living room or a bedroom, effectively expanding the usable space beyond the official exclusive area figure.
