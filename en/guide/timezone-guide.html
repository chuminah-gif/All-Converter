What is Daylight Saving Time (DST)?

Daylight Saving Time is a system where clocks are set forward by one hour during the warmer months to make better use of daylight. It's observed in the US, UK, and most of Europe, but not in Korea, Japan, or China.

Northern Hemisphere countries (US, Europe) typically observe DST from around March to November, while Southern Hemisphere countries (like Australia) observe it in the opposite months, roughly October to April.

Frequently asked questions

Q. What should I watch out for when scheduling a video call?

The time difference can shift by an hour depending on whether the other country is currently observing daylight saving time. It's safest to check the exact time using a time zone converter for the specific date you're scheduling.

Q. Can the date change too, not just the time?

Yes. With large time differences, adding or subtracting hours can push the date to the previous or next day. Our converter shows the date along with the time for exactly this reason.
