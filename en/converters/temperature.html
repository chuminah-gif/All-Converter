About temperature units

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32, and K = °C + 273.15 — these exact formulas are used for every conversion.

Celsius (°C)Fahrenheit (°F)Kelvin (K)
0°C (water freezes)32°F273.15K
100°C (water boils)212°F373.15K
36.5°C (avg. body temp)97.7°F309.65K
-40°C-40°F233.15K

Note

The US commonly uses Fahrenheit for everyday purposes like weather forecasts, which can be confusing for visitors used to Celsius. Kelvin is mainly used in scientific and engineering contexts for absolute temperature.
