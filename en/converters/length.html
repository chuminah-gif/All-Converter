About length units

Length conversions use internationally defined fixed constants, so there's no margin of error. One inch is exactly 2.54cm, and one mile is exactly 1,609.344m.

UnitEquals (in meters)
Inch (in)0.0254 m
Foot (ft)0.3048 m
Yard (yd)0.9144 m
Mile (mi)1,609.344 m

Common examples

  • 1m ≈ 39.37 inches ≈ 3.28 feet
  • A height of "5 foot 7" is about 170.18cm.
  • A marathon (42.195km) is about 26.22 miles.
