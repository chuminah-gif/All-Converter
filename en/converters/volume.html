About volume units

US customary volume units are calculated using exact international definitions. One US gallon is exactly 3.785411784L.

UnitEquals (in L)
US Gallon (gal)3.7854 L
US Quart (qt)0.9464 L
US Cup0.2366 L
US Fluid Ounce (fl oz)0.02957 L

Note

UK (imperial) gallons and pints have different volumes than US units. This converter uses US customary measurements, the most common in international recipes and cross-border shopping.
