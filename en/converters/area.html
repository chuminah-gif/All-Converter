About area units

One pyeong is exactly 3.305785m² and is commonly used for apartment and house sizes in Korea. One acre is 4,046.86m², and one hectare is 10,000m².

UnitEquals (in m²)
Pyeong3.3058 m²
Square foot (ft²)0.0929 m²
Acre4,046.86 m²
Hectare (ha)10,000 m²

Common examples

  • A common Korean apartment size, "34 pyeong," is about 112.4m².
  • US real estate listings are typically given in square feet (sqft).
