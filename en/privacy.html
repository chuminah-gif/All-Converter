Privacy Policy

All Converter (the "Site") values your privacy and complies with applicable law.

Published: July 26, 2026  |  Effective: July 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect and How

The Site is a free converter service that requires no signup. Values you enter into any converter are never sent to a server or stored — they are calculated entirely within your browser and disappear once you leave the page.

The Site does not operate a member database, and contact is handled by email only, subject to the policies of the email service you use (e.g. Gmail).

Access logs, cookies, and IP address information may be automatically collected as part of running the Site and serving ads.

2. Purpose of Collection and Use

  • Operating and improving the Site
  • Preventing fraud and abuse, and ensuring service stability

3. Cookies and Advertising

The Site may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, to serve ads relevant to you.

  • Third-party ad providers such as Google use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising cookies at Google's Ad Settings page.
  • For more on how third-party vendors and ad networks use cookies, see Google's Advertising Policies.
  • You may disable or delete cookies through your browser settings, though this may limit some functionality.

4. Retention Period

Values entered into converters are not collected or retained. Automatically collected information such as access logs is retained only as required by law or for the minimum period necessary to operate the Site.

5. Sharing With Third Parties

The Site does not share your personal information with outside parties, except where necessary for the advertising services described in Section 3, such as with Google.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information the Site holds about you at any time, via the email address listed on the Contact page.
