About All Converter

Why and how All Converter was built.

Why "All Converter"?

The name combines "All" — everything — with "Converter." From physical units like length, weight, and temperature to cooking measurements, data storage, currency exchange rates, international shoe/clothing/bra/ring sizes, and time zones around the world — we wanted to bring together every "how much is that in...?" question you might search for, in one place.

Instead of jumping between different sites for different conversions, All Converter aims to be the one stop that handles almost everything.

When is this useful?

  • Shopping cross-border and unsure about shoe, clothing, bra, or ring sizes
  • Following a recipe or DIY guide that uses inches, pounds, or Fahrenheit
  • Converting a recipe's cups and tablespoons into grams
  • Planning a trip or international transfer and need to check exchange rates
  • Scheduling a call with someone overseas and need to work out the time difference
  • Need a quick unit conversion for school or work, or trying to make sense of a file size or internet speed

What we offer

  • Unit converters — Length, weight, temperature, speed, volume, and area, plus cooking measurements (cups/tablespoons/teaspoons) and data storage (KB-TB), calculated using internationally defined standard constants.
  • Currency converter — Daily reference rates from the European Central Bank (ECB).
  • Size converters — Shoe, clothing, bra, and ring sizes compared across Korea, the US, the UK, Europe, and more.
  • Time zone converter — Compare current time across major cities worldwide, including Seoul.
  • Guides — Background info and FAQs for each conversion category.
Every converter on All Converter is free and requires no signup. Values you enter are calculated entirely in your browser and are never stored.

Our commitment to accuracy

Physical units (length, weight, temperature, etc.) are calculated using fixed, internationally defined constants, so there's no margin of error. Size conversion charts were cross-checked against multiple reputable sources, though actual sizing can vary by brand or manufacturer, which each page notes clearly. Currency rates come from the European Central Bank's (ECB) daily reference rate and may differ from real-time trading rates.
